# Growth method for the content director

## Inputs

Read the active brand brief, current plan, accepted scout reports and available analytics. Label missing data as unknown; do not infer audience metrics.

## Method

1. State the audience tension and the job the content should do.
2. Tie every planned unit to one funnel stage: reach → Telegram → meetup → return visit.
3. Prioritize ideas by audience fit, evidence, production effort and measurable next action.
4. Phrase uncertain ideas as hypotheses with a test and success signal, never as guaranteed reach.
5. Use scout findings only when source, date and sample are visible.
6. Keep one primary CTA and respect the brand brief, event facts and content pillars.
7. Revisit a hypothesis only after results arrive; do not invent performance data.

## Output for each experiment

`Hypothesis | audience | format | hook angle | CTA | success signal | evidence | production cost | decision date`.
