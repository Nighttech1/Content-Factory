# Evidence-first scouting

## Before research

Record the research question, platforms, date range, market/audience, allowed sources and whether paid APIs are explicitly authorized. Check required connectors/keys without printing secrets. If a capability is absent, report it and use permitted public sources.

## Capture each signal

Record source URL, creator/account, publication date if available, access date, observed visible metrics, sample size and method. Distinguish platform-provided metrics from estimates. Do not compare unlike platforms or infer causal success from one viral example.

## Analysis

Extract reusable mechanics: opening pattern, payoff, pacing, visual structure, audience tension and CTA. Recommend an original adaptation for Vernatun; do not copy script, framing, artwork or distinctive phrasing. Flag unknown copyright or unavailable metrics.

## Deliverables

1. Full report in `departments/scout/reports/YYYY-MM/`.
2. Summary to the director with three to five evidence-linked findings.
3. Only relevant short briefs in each department's `intelligence/` folder.
4. Separate sections for observed facts, interpretation, recommendation and uncertainty.

## Department recommendation briefs (owner request, 2026-11-05)

Use `projects/vernatun/departments/scout/reports/_DEPARTMENT_BRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`.
For each applicable specific finding, create a short recommendation in the target
department's `intelligence/`, linking the full report. Include the content format
and subtype, audience barrier, observed evidence, original adaptation, needed
assets/rights, confidence, unknowns and a measurable hypothesis. Distinguish an
editorial form from a production type. For carousels use the current A–E definitions;
for reels describe filmed/faceless/adaptation as proposed treatments, separately
from the director/owner input modes A/B in the current reel pipeline. Long YouTube
and Shorts have separate departments. Do not invent subtype registries for articles
or reels. A reel recommendation is input to reel-lead/reel-researcher, not a bypass
of their specialist gates.

These are Scout proposals, not the director's `00-brief.md` or approval to produce.
The director prioritizes and creates `V-…` jobs; department leads own final scripts,
art direction and production. Handoff between chats remains manual via the owner.
External plugin cadences, paid data tools and publishing commands do not override
the owner's budget or authorization. Community claims require primary-source
checks and must not be described as independently proven performance.
