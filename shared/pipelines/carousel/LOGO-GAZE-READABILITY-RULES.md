# Carousel visual QA addendum — Vernatun

This addendum is mandatory for every carousel.

## Character gaze

- When the heroine is speaking with a person, her eyes must point to that person's face/eyes.
- “Looking at the ceiling” or into empty space is rejected unless the storyline explicitly requires looking at an object there.
- The art director records an eye-line note for every slide and rejects backgrounds that fail it.

## Type 1 dictionary readability

Every middle card has exactly three visible lines:

1. Armenian script.
2. Armenian transliteration in Latin letters.
3. Russian translation.

The three lines must be readable at full size and mobile scale. Any clipped, low-contrast, overlapping or too-thin translation returns to bake.

## Approved logos only

Use only these transparent assets:

- `vernatun-logo-dark.png` — colour emblem + black wordmark.
- `vernatun-logo-light.png` — colour emblem + white wordmark.

Selection is based on the actual top-right background patch. The logo is placed as high and as far right as the safe-zone allows. Preserve proportions, transparency and native emblem-to-wordmark spacing.

Forbidden: generated logo, redraw, plate, circle, frame, shadow, background, white rectangle or extra crop halo.

The bake agent checks the final PNG at full size and mobile scale. `VERNATUN` and `ARMENIAN CLUB` must read immediately and may not cover a face, key text or important object.

## Status

A failed check is `RETURN_TO_FIXER`, never a client delivery.
